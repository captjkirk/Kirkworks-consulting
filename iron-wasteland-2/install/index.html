Iron Wasteland 2 · Mac app

Install it on your Mac.

Five steps, and you only do them once. Your Mac will show a warning in step 3. That is expected: the game is free, and Apple's check for apps costs $99 a year, so the app doesn't have it.

  1. Download the game

    Click the button. A file called Iron-Wasteland-2-mac-universal.zip goes to your Downloads folder. It works on every Mac, Apple chip or Intel.

    Download for Mac

    It is about 250 MB, so give it a minute.

  2. Put it in Applications

    Double-click the zip in your Downloads folder. A file called Iron Wasteland 2 appears. Open Finder, click Applications in the left side, and drag Iron Wasteland 2 into that window.

  3. Open it once, and click Done

    Open the Applications folder and double-click Iron Wasteland 2. This message appears. It is supposed to.

    Do not click Move to Trash. That deletes the game.

  4. Tell your Mac it is okay

    Now open the Mac's settings page for this. Click the button, then click Open if your browser asks to open System Settings.

    Open Privacy & Security

    If the button does nothing, open System Settings, then click Privacy & Security in the left side.

    Scroll all the way down to the Security part. You will see a line saying Iron Wasteland 2 was blocked. Click Open Anyway, then type your Mac's password or use Touch ID.

    Don't see the line? It only stays for about an hour. Go back to step 3, double-click the game again, click Done, and come back here.

  5. Let it find your family's games

    The game opens and asks to find devices on your local network. Click Allow. That is how games in the same house find each other.

    That's it. Next time, just open Iron Wasteland 2 from Applications, with no warning. The game updates itself each time you open it.

On a Mac with macOS 14 or older, skip step 4: right-click Iron Wasteland 2 in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again.
